packaging and thin-to-zero on TikTok, Instagram, X, Bluesky, and Threads mechanics —
no caption-length norms, no hashtag-count conventions, no algorithm behavior, no
posting-time data for any of those four surfaces `[gap]` (per
`.claude/skills/social-repurpose/references/cross-platform-captions.md`). **Every
cross-platform caption below is therefore either `[C→I]` — a YouTube packaging
principle extrapolated `[I]` to that surface — or flagged `[gap]` where no principle
applies at all.** Only the YouTube section below sits on corpus-dense ground.
Specifically: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Bluesky, Threads, and X mechanics (character
limits, hashtag counts, posting cadence, algorithm behavior) are `[gap]` throughout —
not covered by the corpus, not invented here.
