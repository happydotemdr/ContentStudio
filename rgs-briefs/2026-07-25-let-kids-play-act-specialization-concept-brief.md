# Concept Brief: The Trade Nobody Names

## Angle / take
For parents of a multi-sport-capable kid currently being nudged (by a club, a coach, or their
own comparison anxiety) toward cutting everything down to one sport, this Short names the actual
trade being made — not "specializing is risky" (already common advice) but the sharper,
counter-intuitive finding that the specific future payoff families are trading a childhood for
(the scholarship shot, the elite outcome) frequently doesn't arrive even when a family does
everything the specialization path asks of it.
Derived as the same format-collision "purple cow" RGS already proved on this topic cluster —
century-old-thinker-names-the-modern-pattern — paired for the first time with a *reversal*
finding (early specialization predicts junior success but not senior/elite success) rather than
the more commonly-covered injury-risk angle, using a pending federal bill's timeliness as the
reason to publish now without making the bill itself the subject.

## Hook concept
- Emotional trigger: fear/loss-aversion, via the Warning ("stop/don't/never") title frame
- The promise: the opening must reveal, within the first beat, that the "payoff" families are
  cutting other sports and other kids' time for often doesn't show up even on its own terms —
  using the research's junior-success/senior-success reversal, not a generic injury-risk claim —
  before any thinker framing or bill reference arrives.
