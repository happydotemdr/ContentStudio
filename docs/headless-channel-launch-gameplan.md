# Headless YouTube Channel — Launch Game Plan

A concrete, phased roadmap for **one solo operator** starting a brand-new, niche-undecided, **faceless/headless** YouTube channel. The strategy is **Shorts-first, long-form later**: use short-form's tight, fast feedback loops to learn the fundamentals and build a core audience, then layer in long-form for depth, watch-time, and the monetization paths Shorts alone can't reach. `[C]` Beginners should sprint 3–6 months on short-form to build fundamentals before attempting long-form YouTube, because short-form has tighter data feedback loops and faster learning while YouTube is very hard to start cold (Kallaway, ceRZVxO8KF8).

**How to use this alongside the other two docs:**
- **This file** = the *sequence* — decide the niche, stand up the channel, run the sprint, expand.
- **`headless-shorts-production-playbook.md`** = the *how* — the per-video production SOP (scripting, hooks, retention, voiceover, captions, batching). This plan points to it at every production step instead of repeating it.
- **`headless-youtube-audit.md`** = the *guardrails* — the channel-health, rights, and compliance audit checklist. This plan cross-references it in Phase 1 and in the Rights & Policy gate.

**Provenance tags used throughout:**
- `[C]` = corpus finding from the creator-video research set, cited `(Channel, video_id)`.
- `[I]` = general industry practice (not a corpus finding).
- `[T]` = tool- or policy-fact from the web-verified notes file, **verified as of 2026-07-23** (re-verify before relying on any price/threshold later).

## Phase 0 — Niche selection & validation

Do not skip this. `[C]` When videos fail, the real problem is almost always the *idea/niche*, not the hook or editing (Kallaway, i7upRL4H1FM). The single most expensive mistake a faceless operator makes is starting production before the niche is validated.

### The decision framework (from the corpus)

**1. Reject the "ghost niche" (no-competition) trap.** `[C]` Competition *validates* demand; niches with almost no competition usually lack proven buyers and are the hardest to succeed in — you want to see other channels already pulling 50k–500k views on individual videos (Make Money Matt, n7KG9AvdB-s). `[C]` A "saturated" niche is a green light, not a warning: low-competition niches are low-competition because there's no money there (One Person Business, oJHSWmZcsgE).

**2. Go narrow — "niche-quilting" / plant one flag.** `[C]` Own the *smallest* category that still aligns with your offer rather than a broad personality niche (Kallaway, DFSU-YPRTs8). `[C]` The narrower the category, the faster you build strong association for the same amount of content produced — every extra topic splits the content-minutes that deepen your core association (Kallaway, wKdElDeMXR0). `[C]` Choose a hyper-specific sub-niche by targeting a specific demographic or life stage — e.g. "personal finance for people approaching retirement," "creatine after 50" (Make Money Matt, n7KG9AvdB-s). `[C]` But don't *over*-narrow to where the total addressable audience is tiny (Nick Nimmin, la031ela7Sw) — a topic's ceiling is the number of people interested in it (Nick Nimmin, LAzYEKltBwA).

**3. Demand-validate in under 60 seconds.** `[C]` Validate a niche against three questions — *is YouTube okay with this content, is there a paying audience, will it still exist in 5 years* — and check for buyers by opening incognito, searching the niche, opening the top 3 videos and seeing whether the ads sell something related (Make Money Matt, qkYDP4TCkgc).

**4. Weight monetization potential and RPM.** `[C]` Prefer higher-CPM niches because CPM is a proxy for how purchase-ready the audience is — a low-CPM niche may pay ~$2/1,000 views while a high-CPM niche monetized correctly makes $30–50+/1,000 (Make Money Matt, n7KG9AvdB-s). `[C]` Pick a **problem-rich** niche in money, health, relationships, travel, or technology rather than broad entertainment — these contain expensive problems people pay to solve, so you can monetize by *selling*, not just ad revenue (Make Money Matt, TvJhpOxFRsE). `[C]` The "holy trinity" of high-profit niches supports long videos, attracts 40+ viewers, and draws a Western (US/UK/CA/AU) audience (One Person Business, s2knfD7QuCM).

**5. Pick evergreen over trending.** `[C]` Choose evergreen topics so videos keep earning for years; news-based AI channels spike then decay (Make Money Matt, n7KG9AvdB-s).

**6. Faceless-feasibility & obsession.** `[C]` Pick a niche you're genuinely obsessed with (or willing to become obsessed with for a decade) — obsession is the durable input AI cannot clone and the fuel for the volume required (Kallaway, 46t_cn0lx2E). For a faceless channel, also confirm the topic can be *shown* without your face (screen-record, AI visuals, stock, b-roll, text-over-image) and **without reused third-party footage** (see the Rights gate).

**7. Avoid the AI-slop race to the bottom.** `[C]` AI is commoditizing generic entertainment/AI-slop content — don't compete there; the hedge is genuinely valuable content (Make Money Matt, nMLLPNj585I). `[C]` A member's 7 copy-heavy "AI slop" channels failed for ~10 months until channel 8 succeeded once he focused on real audience value (Romayroh, OrPYWlXMQws).

### Niche Decision Worksheet

Score each candidate niche 1–5 on every criterion (5 = best). **Weighted total** guides the pick; treat a **1 or 2 on any single criterion as a veto to investigate**, not just a low average.

| Criterion | What a 5 looks like | Anchor (corpus) |
|---|---|---|
| **Passion / knowledge** | You could make videos on this for years without burning out | Obsession is the un-clonable input (Kallaway, 46t_cn0lx2E) `[C]` |
| **Search + browse demand** | Multiple channels hit 50k+ views on single videos; clear interest volume | Competition validates demand (Make Money Matt, n7KG9AvdB-s) `[C]` |
| **Monetization / RPM** | High CPM + an obvious paid off-ramp (course, digital product, high-ticket affiliate) | Problem-rich niches, RPM as buyer proxy (Make Money Matt, TvJhpOxFRsE / n7KG9AvdB-s) `[C]` |
| **Competition gap** | Proven demand *plus* an un-owned angle you can remix into | Enter a proven niche with a remixed angle (One Person Business, oJHSWmZcsgE) `[C]` |
| **Faceless-feasibility** | Fully makeable with AI voice + AI/stock/screen visuals, no reused footage | AI voiceover is fine; reused footage is the trap (`[T]` + One Person Business, eVePkmCQV5c) |
| **Ability to batch** | Repeatable format; one video producible in a short, fixed time | "Simple scales, fancy fails" (One Person Business, s2knfD7QuCM) `[C]` |

**Worked example row** (illustrative candidate: *"Personal-finance micro-lessons for people 50+ planning retirement"*):

| Niche | Passion | Demand | Monet/RPM | Comp gap | Faceless | Batch | **Total /30** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retirement finance 50+ | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | **27** — strong: high RPM finance audience, proven demand, easily faceless (screen + AI VO), remixable angle for the 50+ life-stage |
| (Candidate B) Generic "motivation" quotes | 3 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 5 | 5 | **20 — veto**: RPM low and comp-gap ~1 (saturated AI-slop lane), exactly the race-to-the-bottom to avoid |

Pick the highest-scoring candidate with **no veto**. Lock it before Phase 1. `[I]` Write a one-sentence positioning statement — *"I make [format] for [specific viewer] so they can [outcome]"* — and keep it visible on your desk.

## Phase 1 — Channel foundation

Checklist form. Cross-reference **`headless-youtube-audit.md`** for the full channel-health/settings audit; the security and settings items below are the launch-critical subset.

### Naming & positioning
- [ ] `[C]` Before naming, confirm the name isn't taken and check the **WIPO global brand database** for a registered trademark (Make Money Matt, LGYiPA5SKvw).
- [ ] `[C]` Make video-level optimization consistent with channel-level — description, keywords, and playlists all reinforce **one** niche (Dan the creator, bTr-Izh9pkc).
- [ ] `[C]` Write channel keywords as **5–10 relevant, comma-separated** terms (not hashtags), no spam (One Person Business, eVePkmCQV5c / Romayroh, Wox4Jt_2t6w).
- [ ] `[C]` Build the channel "like a show": one consistent thumbnail/cover style, color, and font (One Person Business, eVePkmCQV5c).

### Handle, art & account hardening
- [ ] `[C]` Set **not-made-for-kids**, country = US (or your tier-1 market), feature eligibility enabled (One Person Business, eVePkmCQV5c).
- [ ] `[C]` **Verify the channel is human** (ID upload *or* ~1 month of history) to unlock description links, custom thumbnails, and larger promotion (Make Money Matt, tqCMF3mI9Pg).
- [ ] `[T]` Enable **2-step verification** — it's a hard prerequisite for the Partner Program (verified 2026-07-23).
- [ ] `[C]` Use a **separate public/sponsor-contact email**, never your channel's Google login email (Make Money Matt, 10yFPNpnjY0 / Dan the creator, Wx5gF0jkgZI).
- [ ] `[C]` Set videos to **Standard YouTube License**, not Creative Commons (Nick Nimmin, WoU8ri3GTsU).
- [ ] `[C]` Turn **off** third-party AI-training on your content; turn **off** Shorts remixing (set to audio-only) to prevent AI-cloning of your assets (Dan the creator, I5V7283Qxf4 / vidIQ, 7R8C2rRO0qA).
- [ ] `[C]` (Optional, entity-building) Create matching-name off-platform accounts (IG, X, Facebook, Pinterest, LinkedIn) and cross-link them so you read as a *brand entity*, not an anonymous uploader (Romayroh, ErCV5czVK1g).

### Tool stack to set up
Set up the minimum viable stack now; the **`headless-shorts-production-playbook.md`** covers configuration and the daily workflow. Don't over-buy — `[C]` $200–300/month of overlapping AI tools is unnecessary; you need roughly thumbnails + scripts + voice + editing (Romayroh, nFT1xNDprIk).
- **Editing + captions:** `[T]` CapCut — best free editor with auto-captions; primary $0 start (verified 2026-07-23). `[T]` Upgrade to Submagic (~$23/mo annual) for strongest animated captions when budget allows.
- **AI voiceover:** `[T]` ElevenLabs (quality leader, ~$22/mo Creator) or a budget path (Murf Falcon API ~$0.01/min, Fish Audio, or free/local Chatterbox) (verified 2026-07-23).
- **Thumbnails / covers:** `[T]` Canva (fast templates) + Ideogram 3.0 when the image needs reliable on-image **text** (verified 2026-07-23).
- **Research/optimization:** `[T]` vidIQ or TubeBuddy for keyword/competitor/A-B-title work; analytics = YouTube Studio Advanced mode (verified 2026-07-23).

### Banking / AdSense prerequisites
- [ ] `[T]` Prepare for the linked **AdSense** account required at Partner-Program acceptance (verified 2026-07-23). `[I]` Have your legal name, address, and a payout bank account ready so acceptance isn't delayed when you hit the threshold.
- [ ] **Do not enter banking/AdSense credentials on anyone's behalf** — the operator completes AdSense sign-up and banking personally.

## Phase 2 — Shorts sprint (Days 0–90)

The goal of the first 90 days is **learning velocity + a core audience**, not revenue. Publish narrow, consistent Shorts to one avatar; read the data; iterate.

### Cadence & volume
- `[C]` Warm up a brand-new account ~3 days by engaging as a real human before your first post (One Person Business, 84bavOadYCI).
- `[C]` In the early **skill-acquisition** phase, make one video, post it, *analyze it, and learn* before making the next — do **not** batch yet, because batching delays the feedback you need (Kallaway, DFSU-YPRTs8). Move to batching only once the format stabilizes.
- `[C]` Target roughly **3+ videos/week** as the sustainable consistency signal (Make Money Matt, tqCMF3mI9Pg); some Shorts channels post daily. `[C]` Make videos stupid-simple to produce so you can stay consistent — "simple scales, fancy fails" (One Person Business, s2knfD7QuCM).
- `[C]` Once you're batching, run **batches of 10** holding 4 of 5 components constant (format, idea, hook, script, visuals) and changing one; find the 5×/10× outliers and double down (Kallaway, B9l9TRhu5Vw).

### The production loop
Per-video execution lives in **`headless-shorts-production-playbook.md`** (hook → retention → script → voiceover → captions → cover). `[C]` Schedule each *part* of the job (ideas, research, scripting, recording/assembly, editing) into your week rather than working only when you have spare time (Nick Nimmin, kcSOFqJhR9I).

### Ideation for the sprint
- `[C]` Build every Short around **one topic and one takeaway** — cramming 3–5 ideas dilutes it (Kallaway, i7upRL4H1FM).
- `[C]` Use **niche-bending / transpositioning**: import a format proven in *another* niche into yours before anyone else does, rather than copying within your niche (Dan the creator, oWUG6gwWrME / Nick Nimmin, h8OTdq24irE).
- `[C]` Build around a **contrarian take** — state the common belief, introduce your frame, explain why yours is right (Kallaway, 6G1wIdxWF5w). `[C]` Study **small/medium** creators in your niche, not mega accounts, when reverse-engineering (Dan the creator, Wx5gF0jkgZI / Kallaway, o_n5ED5fE1I).

### The analytics feedback loop (what to watch + iterate rule)
- `[C]` Diagnose zero-views by **impressions, not view count**: impressions with low CTR = a packaging (title/cover) problem; near-zero impressions = the algorithm doesn't yet know who to show it to, i.e. a targeting/clarity problem (One Person Business, s2knfD7QuCM).
- `[I]` For Shorts specifically, watch **swipe-away rate** (are people leaving in the first 1–2 seconds → hook problem), **retention curve / % viewed** (mid-video drop → pacing/payoff problem), and **average view duration (AVD)**.
- `[C]` Regularly feed your best- vs worst-performing videos' transcripts and metrics into an LLM to surface non-obvious patterns to double down on (Kallaway, LvuoNlYRs7g).
- **Iterate rule:** change **one variable at a time** against the batch, keep what beats the batch average, kill what doesn't (Kallaway, B9l9TRhu5Vw) `[C]`. `[C]` Accept that **88% of videos never reach 1,000 lifetime views** — flops are the normal cost of finding the outliers (Roberto Blake, q64Iczdhb-Y).

### 90-day milestone table

| Weeks | Focus | Output target | Primary metric to move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0–2** | Account warm-up; publish first Shorts **one at a time**, analyze each | 3–4 Shorts/wk, no batching | Are impressions being served at all? (targeting clarity) |
| **3–4** | Lock a repeatable format + cover style; test hooks | 3–5 Shorts/wk | Swipe-away rate ↓ (first-2-second hold) |
| **5–8** | Begin **batches of 10**, one variable changed per batch | 3–5+/wk, batched | % viewed / AVD ↑; find first 5×–10× outlier |
| **9–12** | Double down on outlier formats; prune off-avatar experiments | Steady 4–7/wk | Subscriber velocity + returning-viewer signal |

**End-of-sprint checkpoint:** you should have a proven format, a cover/voice style, an identified outlier or two, and early subscriber momentum — the trigger conditions for Phase 3.

## Phase 3 — Long-form expansion

Add long-form once Shorts have taught you the fundamentals — not before.

### Trigger criteria (any two, ideally all)
- You have a **repeatable, outlier-validated Short format** and a clear avatar.
- Sustained cadence of **3+/week** without burning out.
- Early subscriber momentum + a **monetization off-ramp** ready to point long-form at.
- `[C]` (Kallaway, ceRZVxO8KF8) frames this as: sprint short-form 3–6 months to build fundamentals, then *add or swap to* long-form.

### How to repurpose (both directions)
- **Short → long:** cluster several winning Shorts on one sub-topic into a single deeper long-form video (the "information DJ" move — `[C]` watch the top 3 videos at 2×, bullet them, mine comments for gaps, remix into a more comprehensive video (Make Money Matt, xZfM93XGy0g)). `[T]` Opus Clip / Submagic Magic Clips automate the long→short direction (verified 2026-07-23).
- **Long → Shorts:** slice each long-form video into multiple Shorts as top-of-funnel that point back to the long-form.
- `[C]` **Session time** is the payoff: directing viewers to a 30–60-minute video keeps them on-platform, which YouTube rewards with more recommendations (Make Money Matt, xZfM93XGy0g).

### How the workflow scales
- `[C]` Longer videos reach the 4,000-hour threshold far faster — a 10-min video needs ~80k views vs ~266k for a 3-min video (Dan the creator, SnrciZs-OmM). `[C]` Aim ~12–16-min videos at a 3–4-min AVD (Roberto Blake, 7rqw6rVClWo).
- `[C]` Organize into **content buckets**, each backed by its own **playlist**; add every upload to its most relevant playlist and build **content bridges / end screens** so viewers binge (Dan the creator, SnrciZs-OmM / v562jH_TESg / mmUFA_Nnsm4).
- `[C]` Build **pillar / cornerstone** videos that every other video funnels to (Roberto Blake, InN8JOCRyXI / Make Money Matt, 66xRIhy6XWs).
- `[C]` Right-size effort — as a solo/"working-class" creator cap each long-form video at ~5–10 hours so you sustain 1–2/week (Roberto Blake, 4uSOnI0v8U0). `[C]` Expect the top ~20% of videos to drive ~80% of views, so keep making them (Roberto Blake, q64Iczdhb-Y).

## KPIs & monetization gates

### The verified YPP thresholds `[T]` (as of 2026-07-23)
- **Full YPP:** **1,000 subscribers AND (4,000 public watch-hours in 12 months OR 10,000,000 Shorts views in 90 days)** — plus 2-step verification, no active Community-Guidelines strikes, a linked AdSense account, and an eligible country.
- **Early-access tier (2026):** **500 subscribers + 3 public uploads + (3,000 watch-hours OR 3,000,000 Shorts views) in 90 days** → limited monetization features early.
- **Critical for a Shorts-first channel:** `[T]` **Shorts watch-time does NOT count toward the 4,000-watch-hour path** — Shorts qualify only via the **views** path (10M/90d full, 3M/90d early). This is *the* reason to add long-form for the hour-based route.
- `[C]` Corroboration: never buy ads for views — paid watch-hours don't count toward the 4,000-hour threshold (Dan the creator, SnrciZs-OmM).

### Don't wait for YPP to monetize
- `[C]` Views and subscribers are vanity metrics; income comes from **selling** — a 1,000-sub channel can out-earn a million-sub channel (Make Money Matt, xZfM93XGy0g). `[C]` Sell your own product or an affiliate offer from day one — a $200 course or $49 guide beats $3–10/1,000 AdSense (Make Money Matt, jCluG_I6o8E / One Person Business, s2knfD7QuCM).
- `[C]` Give every video a topic-aligned **off-ramp** (ideally a lead magnet that captures email) — email is ~99.8% deliverable vs 2–3% organic reach (Kallaway, pSIY9ngEZC8 / DSDNiug5EH4). `[C]` Keep viewers *inside* YouTube by funneling Shorts/short videos to a long on-platform pillar rather than linking straight out (Make Money Matt, TvJhpOxFRsE).

### Per-stage KPIs

| Stage | Leading KPI | Lagging KPI | Monetization gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 2 (Shorts sprint)** | Swipe-away ↓, % viewed ↑, outlier hit-rate | Subscriber velocity | (Optional) first digital product / lead magnet live |
| **Early Phase 3** | AVD (long-form), CTR, session time | Watch-hours accruing | Early-access tier: 500 subs + 3k hrs OR 3M Shorts views/90d |
| **Scaling** | Playlist/binge depth, returning viewers | Subs + watch-hours | Full YPP: 1,000 subs + 4k hrs OR 10M Shorts views/90d |

### Review rhythm `[I]`
- **Weekly:** per-video/batch retention + CTR; pick next batch's single variable to test; update end screens on winners.
- **Monthly:** channel-level totals (subs, watch-hours or Shorts-views pace toward the gate), RPM/product revenue, and a winners-vs-losers LLM pattern review (Kallaway, LvuoNlYRs7g) `[C]`.

## Rights & policy gate (must-read before publishing)

**This is the #1 faceless-channel demonetization trap.** Read this before your first upload and re-check it at every batch. Cross-reference **`headless-youtube-audit.md`** for the full rights/compliance audit.

### The two policies (independent — you must pass *both*) `[T]` (as of 2026-07-23)
- **"Inauthentic content"** (renamed from "repetitious content" 2025-07-15): targets **mass-produced / templated / trivially-replicable-at-scale** videos with little variation. **Judged channel-wide** — enough of it can demonetize the *whole* channel, not just one video.
- **"Reused content":** copying or minimally modifying **someone else's** content without adding sufficient original value; enforcement tightened in 2026. Compliance with the two is **independent** — you can pass AI-disclosure yet fail reused-content, and vice-versa.

### Faceless & AI are NOT banned — here's the line `[T]`
- **Faceless is not a banned category.** Channels with **original scripts, real curation/research, and a consistent style** stay monetization-eligible. What kills channels is *mass-produced templated AI slop with no original input.*
- **AI voiceover alone does NOT require disclosure** and is not disqualifying. **AI disclosure** is required only when AI *meaningfully alters or generates realistic* content (e.g. a synthetic likeness of a real person). Disclosing synthetic content does **not** by itself reduce reach or monetization.

### How to stay on the right side (do this on every video)
- **Original scripts + meaningful variation:** write your own script and structure; don't run one template with only superficial swaps across dozens of videos. `[C]` Avoid the exact formats YouTube targets — mass-produced Reddit-story videos, fake trailers, fake true-crime, AI kids content, near-duplicate repetitious videos (Make Money Matt, tqCMF3mI9Pg). `[C]` The July-2025 update targets mass-produced/repetitive spam, **not** faceless or AI-assisted creators who add real input (Nick Nimmin, Ec5RFkpN7Co).
- **Real curation, not reused footage:** `[C]` Don't build faceless videos from other people's reused footage — copyright claims can seize your revenue and demonetize you (One Person Business, eVePkmCQV5c). `[C]` AI content stays monetizable as long as it isn't pure brainrot slop or a recreation of a real person's likeness (One Person Business, 6s2T2NlWDhQ).
- **Never cross-post identical content:** `[C]` Never post the same content across multiple channels — it gets you terminated (Romayroh, Wox4Jt_2t6w). Add original value; don't minimally-modify.
- **Music/footage licensing:** `[C]` Use a licensing service (e.g. Lickd) for famous tracks; note YouTube's free Creator Music can mean revenue-sharing/no-monetization on long-form (Roberto Blake, SJsGBKGy4Do). `[C]` Use only short 5–7s clips even from partnered archives (One Person Business, phQOB0yLNZ0).
- **Disclosures & labels:** `[C]` Check the **altered/AI-content** box when using an AI clone of a real person (small disclosure, no performance impact), mark **not-made-for-kids**, and add finance/health + affiliate disclaimers (Make Money Matt, 10yFPNpnjY0). `[C]` **Never make specific income claims** ("$500/day guaranteed") in titles/thumbnails — that draws strikes (Make Money Matt, nMLLPNj585I).
- **Ongoing hygiene (audit doc):** `[C]` Keep the Studio **restrictions** column reading "none" on every video (Dan the creator, JPTr40J3WXU); keep the login email private (Dan the creator, Wx5gF0jkgZI); turn off AI-training and Shorts-remix (Dan the creator, I5V7283Qxf4 / vidIQ, 7R8C2rRO0qA); and never click DM/PDF "brand deal," fake-copyright, or "private video shared with you" phishing — legitimate brands and claims use proper email and name the specific track/video (Nick Nimmin, gVvhDS5td4Y / Dan the creator, fSnPbJfudhs).

**The one-line rule:** *original script + real curation + meaningful variation per video = monetization-safe faceless channel.* AI voice, AI visuals, and no-face are all fine; templated slop and reused footage are what get channels demonetized or terminated.
