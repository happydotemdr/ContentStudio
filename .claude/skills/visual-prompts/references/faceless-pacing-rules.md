# Faceless-Short visual pacing — distilled from the audit's §6 (thin corpus, 27 findings)

Distilled from `docs/headless-youtube-audit.md` §6 "Visuals & AI assets." The audit itself flags this
as **another thin theme (27 findings)** — the corpus favors simple, high-value visuals over heavy AI
production, and repeatedly warns that AI thumbnails/B-roll still hit the uncanny valley. Treat these as
directional, high-consensus rules rather than a deep production manual — where the corpus doesn't give
a specific number or method, say so instead of inventing one.

## The cadence rule this skill is built around

- **Change the on-screen visual every ~3 seconds; never hold one image/clip too long** `[C]`
  `[C] (Make Money Matt, HopTPCLbiiM)`.
- **Match the visual to what's being said, sentence-by-sentence** `[C]` — or show plain footage with no
  overlay. A random, mismatched visual causes confusion and viewer drop-off `[C] (Kallaway, i7upRL4H1FM)`.
- **A static, unchanging frame is "the visual equivalent of dead air"** `[C] (vidIQ, DiZnbihU4NM)`.
- **Subtle slow zoom/pan (keyframe scale ~110-120%) adds a premium feel** `[C]`, used mostly on early scenes
  — but note most exploding channels just cut fast instead of relying on pan/zoom
  `[C] (One Person Business, eVePkmCQV5c; vidIQ, DiZnbihU4NM)`.

## Don't over-produce

- **Don't over-edit** `[C]` — flashbangs, stacked overlays, and constant jump cuts exhaust viewers.
  Comprehension comes from *deleting* edits; rawer/simpler editing increasingly reads as more authentic
  and less AI-generated — **strongly-supported** (two channels agree)
  `[C] (Kallaway, i7upRL4H1FM; Nate Black, J8LrrCpDNJI)`.
- **Match editing pace to the audience** `[C]` — faster for younger viewers, slower for older/learning
  audiences `[C] (Nick Nimmin, LAzYEKltBwA)`.
- **Retention spikes on genuine value delivery** `[C]` (screen recordings, walkthroughs) beat any jump cut
  `[C] (vidIQ, DiZnbihU4NM)`.

## AI-slop avoidance (why "No Text" and subject-free plates matter beyond the MJ-specific reason)

- **The AI-slop era is closing — use AI to support, not replace, the creator — strongly-supported.** `[C]`
  By late 2025 the feed flooded with low-quality AI content that viewers click off; use AI for
  ideas/research/assets, not to replace judgment, and re-inject human elements (personal stories, real
  proof) `[C] (Dan the creator, 3tkF8cdyXEU; vidIQ, Sgav7Bkg36M; vidIQ, C3GGi8fUvBA)`.
- **AI still fails at thumbnails and pure B-roll** `[C]` due to an uncanny-valley look, as observed in the
  corpus `[C] (Nate Black, 9CCmMypN8PM)`. This is a caution to keep in mind when a prompt sheet leans
  heavily on AI stills for every beat — mix in real stock footage for concrete objects where it's
  available, per the production playbook's own guidance to intercut Storyblocks/Pixabay/Pexels footage.
- **The exploding "AI slideshow" format** `[C]` — static hand-drawn/2D-cinematic images over a voiceover with
  slow pans, scenes changing every 1–5 seconds, no motion needed — is cited as cheap (~$20–40/video) and
  currently pulling large view counts `[C] (One Person Business, Wib4p6bbtGo)`. This is direct support
  for defaulting this skill to **stills** rather than reaching for video generation on every beat.
- **Don't slap an AI talking-head avatar on top unless necessary** `[C]` — it reads as spammy; most exploding
  channels analyzed have no AI host `[C] (One Person Business, Uoav-kiMbM8)`. Counterpoint from the same
  theme: a *recurring named character/avatar* used deliberately as the channel's "face" can resist
  commoditization, because copying a named personality builds the channel's brand rather than diluting
  it `[C] (Make Money Matt, nMLLPNj585I)`. If the incoming script calls for a recurring character, this
  is the corpus backing for building a character reference sheet rather than treating every beat as a
  disposable generic still.

## Where this theme is thin — say so rather than filling the gap

The audit explicitly notes visual-pacing production specifics beyond these bullets "lean on the tool
notes" (i.e. the web-verified `[T]` material in the Midjourney/ElevenLabs guides), not the creator
corpus. Don't present a precise cut-count, shot-duration formula, or "optimal" number of stills per beat
as corpus-derived beyond the "~3 seconds" figure above — anything more granular is this skill's own
operational judgment applied to that one finding, not a separate sourced claim.
