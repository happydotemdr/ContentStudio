## Input (from `shorts-assembly`)

- **Working angle** (from `shorts-ideation`): the Titanic's radio operator, Jack
  Phillips, kept transmitting distress calls past the point of survival — an
  under-covered angle versus the iceberg/lifeboat framing every other Titanic video
  uses.
- **Script hook (first line):** "The last thing Jack Phillips typed wasn't a goodbye —
  it was still a distress signal."
- **Packaging direction inherited from upstream:** thumbnail shows a close crop of a

  thumbnail ("HE KEPT TYPING").
- **Run length:** ~55 seconds, single Short, no long-form companion planned yet.

## The cross-posting caution (include when asked for many platforms at once)

[C] The corpus is itself ambivalent about spreading across this many surfaces at once
— "concentrate violent volume on one platform" (Kallaway, 46t_cn0lx2E) and
over-diversifying before closing the beginner skill gap is cited as a top reason
beginners quit (Kallaway, DFSU-YPRTs8). If this is an early-stage channel, consider
shipping to YouTube plus **one** other platform rather than all four.
